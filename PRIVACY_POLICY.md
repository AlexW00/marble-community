# Marble Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 24, 2026**

Marble is an iPhone and iPad app for recording spending allowances and savings goals. It is developed by Alexander Weichart ("I", "me"). Marble records virtual money: it does not connect to a bank, read financial accounts, or transfer real money.

## Your jar data

Marble stores the jars and bookkeeping entries you create, including jar names, currencies, settings, budgets, savings targets, amounts, entry titles, transaction dates, and recorded history. It also stores participant identifiers, display names, colors, and membership information needed to associate entries with the right person. App preferences, such as appearance, tilt movement, and haptics, are stored on your device.

Marble uses Apple's Core Data for local storage. You do not create a separate Marble account. I do not operate an app backend that receives your jar data, and I cannot read your private or shared CloudKit database through Marble's developer account.

## iCloud synchronization

When Marble has a usable iCloud account, it uses Apple's CloudKit to synchronize account-associated jars between your devices. Without a usable iCloud account, Marble can keep solo jars locally. Existing local-only jars remain separate unless you choose to connect them to iCloud through the app.

CloudKit processes jar content and account-specific record identifiers to provide synchronization and sharing. Apple operates this service under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and applicable iCloud terms. Synchronization can occur after a local save, when a network connection becomes available.

Signing out of or changing your iCloud account removes the previous account's local mirrored cache. Entries that have not synchronized may be lost. Uninstalling Marble or signing out does not itself delete jars already stored in iCloud.

## Shared jars

Sharing is an explicit action. If you invite someone to a jar or accept an invitation, that jar's content becomes available to its participants through CloudKit. This includes balances, entries and their titles and dates, participant display names and colors, and history. Shared visibility includes each person's recorded activity; individual control of an allocation does not make that activity private from the other participants.

Apple's sharing interface may also display Apple account identity information used for invitations and share management. Marble may use the share owner's Apple-provided name to prefill their display name in a shared jar. Do not enter information into a shared jar that you do not want its participants to see.

Leaving a jar or removing a participant revokes that participant's access, but preserves their recorded history for the remaining participants. The jar owner can delete the jar for everyone. Participants may retain information they previously saw or copied, including screenshots; Marble cannot recall those copies.

## Motion and device features

With tilt movement enabled, Marble reads device-motion samples to animate its glass jars and marbles. These samples are processed on the device and are not uploaded by Marble. You can disable tilt movement in Settings. Haptics use supported device hardware and do not record personal information. Playing with the marbles never changes recorded money.

Marble does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or bank accounts for its current features.

## Purchases

Marble Pro purchases and subscriptions use Apple's App Store and StoreKit. Marble reads product information and verified transaction details, such as product and transaction identifiers, expiration, and revocation status, to determine Pro access. Payment details are handled by Apple and are not available to Marble.

Marble does not send StoreKit transaction records to a separate developer-operated server. Apple may provide developers with App Store sales, subscription, and diagnostic reports under its own services and your Apple privacy settings. Marble Pro subscriptions are managed and cancelled through your Apple account; deleting a jar or uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Marble includes no advertising, third-party analytics SDK, or cross-app tracking. It does not sell your data or use it for advertising. Device-motion data, balances, entry titles, and jar history are not transmitted to me for analytics. Apple may process App Store, iCloud, and diagnostic information under its own privacy policies and your system settings.

## Retention, deletion, and your choices

Your jar data remains on your device and, for cloud-associated jars, in iCloud until deleted. Editing or cancelling an entry retains ledger history; it is not an erasure of the original record. Completing a savings goal also retains its balance and history. To remove a jar and its history, its owner must use Delete jar. Minimal local identifiers and completion receipts may remain to prevent deleted jars from reappearing or being processed twice; this bookkeeping excludes entry amounts, titles, and participant profile content. Cloud deletion and removal from other devices depend on Apple's synchronization and retention behavior.

You can leave a shared jar, change its sharing permissions if you own it, disable tilt movement, and manage Marble's iCloud access in Apple system settings. Disabling iCloud access does not erase existing cloud records. Device and iCloud backups may retain earlier copies under your backup settings and Apple's retention rules.

For questions or requests concerning support correspondence or information I hold, contact me below. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of personal information, and to complain to your local data protection authority. I cannot retrieve private CloudKit jar contents on your behalf. Information independently retained by Apple, Fastmail, GitHub, or other jar participants is subject to their applicable policies and responsibilities.

## Children

Marble does not include advertising or knowingly request children's personal information for marketing. Its local-storage and iCloud behavior applies to all users. Please avoid sending children's personal information in public discussions or support attachments.
